VALORANT LOCATIONS

ALPHA EARTH LOCATIONS

ASCENT
Ascent is located in Venice, Italy, on Alpha Earth. It is an important
location connected to the major events surrounding the conflict between
Alpha Earth and Omega Earth.

BIND
Bind is located in Rabat, Morocco, on Alpha Earth. The location is known
for its connection to Kingdom Corporation and contains one-way teleporters.
It also has lore connected to Kingdom activities and communications.

SPLIT
Split is located in Shinjuku, Tokyo, Japan, on Alpha Earth. Its lore shows
a conflict between Kingdom Corporation's development and local residents
who oppose the corporation's expansion.

HAVEN
Haven is located in Thimphu, Bhutan, on Alpha Earth. The location contains
an ancient temple affected by a Radianite-related event. Kingdom Corporation
also became involved with the site because of its Radianite resources.

ICEBOX
Icebox is located on Bennett Island, Russia, on Alpha Earth. It is connected
to Kingdom Corporation and contains lore involving Radianite-related
activities and Kingdom communications.

OMEGA EARTH LOCATIONS

PEARL
Pearl is located in Lisbon, Portugal, on Omega Earth. The city is protected
by a large geo-dome because rising ocean levels threatened to submerge it.
The VALORANT Legion and Kingdom Industries helped protect the city and its
population.

LOTUS
Lotus is located in the Western Ghats of India on Omega Earth. The location
contains important lore connected to ancient structures and activities
investigated by different groups.

CORRODE
Corrode is located at Mont-Saint-Michel, France, on Omega Earth. It contains
lore elements involving communications and activities connected to Atlas.
