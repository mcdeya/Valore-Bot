VALORANT AGENTS

BRIMSTONE
 - Hailing from the United States, Brimstone is a veteran soldier and the first Agent of the VALORANT Protocol. He helped establish the Protocol alongside Viper and serves as its leader. Known as a dependable father figure, he relies on advanced technology and military experience rather than Radiant powers.

VIPER
 - From the United States, Viper is a brilliant scientist and the second Agent of the VALORANT Protocol. She specializes in chemistry and toxic substances and became one of the organization's key recruiters. Her mysterious past and close connection to Omen make her one of the Protocol's most important figures.

OMEN
 - Omen is the third Agent of the VALORANT Protocol and one of its most mysterious members. A past incident left him physically transformed and with much of his memory missing. He is closely connected to Viper and spends much of his story trying to recover information about his past.

KILLJOY
 - Killjoy is a German inventor and the fourth Agent of the Protocol. She is a technological genius who creates advanced weapons, robots, and equipment for the organization. She has a close relationship with Brimstone, who has acted as a mentor to her.

CYPHER
 - Cypher is a Moroccan information broker and the fifth Agent of the Protocol. He specializes in gathering information and uncovering secrets about people and organizations. His surveillance technology makes him one of the Protocol's most effective intelligence specialists.

SOVA
 - Sova is a Russian hunter and the sixth Agent of the Protocol. He is known for his tracking skills, discipline, and ability to locate enemies. His calm personality and loyalty make him a trusted member of Brimstone's team.

SAGE
 - Sage is a Radiant from China and the seventh Agent of the Protocol. She possesses healing and life-related abilities and is known for her calm and caring personality. She is one of the Protocol's trusted members and has a long friendship with Brimstone.

AGENT 08
 - Agent 08 is the mysterious eighth member of the VALORANT Protocol. Their real identity, country, role, and codename remain unknown. The missing number between VP-07 and VP-09 has become an unresolved mystery in VALORANT lore.

PHOENIX
 - Phoenix is a Radiant from the United Kingdom and the ninth Agent of the Protocol. He can manipulate fire and has a confident and energetic personality. Phoenix became involved in the events surrounding the Venice Incident and developed a close friendship with Jett.

JETT
 - Hailing from South Korea, Jett is the tenth Agent of the Protocol and a Radiant who controls wind. She is confident, independent, and prefers doing things her own way. Before joining the Protocol, she was connected to a restaurant in Seoul that was destroyed by a powerful storm associated with her Omega Earth counterpart.

REYNA
 - Reyna is a Radiant from Mexico and the eleventh Agent of the Protocol. Her abilities are connected to the life force of other people, making her extremely powerful in combat. Much of her motivation comes from protecting her younger sister Lucia, whose condition is connected to Reyna's efforts to preserve Radiant life.

RAZE
 - Raze is a Radiant from Brazil and the twelfth Agent of the Protocol. She is an energetic inventor who uses explosive technology in combat. Her creative personality and love of explosions make her one of the most energetic members of the team.

BREACH
 - Breach is a Swedish criminal and the thirteenth Agent of the Protocol. He has mechanical arms and a history involving crime and prison. Despite his rough personality and criminal past, he became a valuable member of the Protocol because of his combat experience and destructive abilities.

SKYE
 - Skye is an Australian Agent and the fourteenth member of the Protocol. She is strongly connected to nature and wildlife and can create animal-like constructs to assist her team. Before joining the Protocol, she was known for protecting the environment and fighting threats to her homeland.

YORU
 - Yoru is a Japanese Radiant and the fifteenth Agent of the Protocol. He can manipulate dimensional rifts and travel between spaces. His connection to his Japanese ancestry and his search for information about his past are important parts of his story.

ASTRA
 - Astra is a Ghanaian Radiant and the sixteenth Agent of the Protocol. She possesses cosmic abilities that allow her to interact with the astral plane and influence events from a different perspective. Her confidence and knowledge of cosmic forces make her one of the Protocol's more unusual Agents.

KAY/O
 - KAY/O is a combat robot from a future affected by the Radiant War. He was created to fight against Radiants during a devastating conflict between humans and Radiants. After traveling through time, KAY/O became part of the Protocol and works to prevent the future war from happening.

CHAMBER
 - Chamber is a French weapons designer and the eighteenth Agent of the Protocol. He specializes in advanced weapons and technology and is known for his confident and secretive personality. His actions and plans are closely connected to the larger conflict between Alpha Earth and Omega Earth.

NEON
 - Neon is a Filipino Radiant and the nineteenth Agent of the Protocol. She can generate and control powerful electrical energy, giving her incredible speed and mobility. Her abilities and knowledge of technology make her an important part of the Protocol's missions.

FADE
 - Fade is a Turkish Agent and the twentieth member of the Protocol. She possesses supernatural abilities connected to fear and nightmares. Before joining the Protocol, she discovered confidential information about the organization and used it to blackmail Brimstone before eventually becoming an Agent herself.

HARBOR
 - Harbor is an Indian Agent and the twenty-first member of the Protocol. His abilities are connected to an ancient artifact that allows him to control water. Before joining the Protocol, he was connected to REALM, an organization involved in recovering and returning important artifacts.

GEKKO
 - Gekko is an American Agent from Los Angeles and the twenty-second member of the Protocol. He works closely with his four creatures, known as his buddies, which assist him in combat. His background, friends, and connection to his creatures are important parts of his character.

DEADLOCK
 - Deadlock is a Norwegian Agent and the twenty-third member of the Protocol. She uses advanced technology and specialized equipment in combat. Her experiences before joining the Protocol shaped her determined personality and made her an important addition to the team.

ISO
 - Iso is a Chinese Agent and the twenty-fourth member of the Protocol. He is a contract killer who can create powerful defensive and offensive effects using his abilities. His professional background and controlled personality make him a dangerous and highly skilled Agent.

CLOVE
 - Clove is a Scottish Radiant and the twenty-fifth Agent of the Protocol. Their abilities are connected to life and death, allowing them to return to the fight even after being killed. Clove has a playful and confident personality and brings a different perspective to the Protocol.

VYSE
 - Vyse is a mysterious Radiant and the twenty-sixth Agent of the Protocol. She uses metallic and plant-like abilities to control areas and trap enemies. Her background and history are closely connected to advanced technology and the wider conflicts of the VALORANT universe.

TEJO
 - Tejo is a Colombian Agent and the twenty-seventh member of the Protocol. He is an intelligence consultant and experienced operative who uses specialized technology and explosives. His background in intelligence makes him useful for planning and executing dangerous missions.

WAYLAY
 - Waylay is a Thai Radiant and the twenty-eighth Agent of the Protocol. She uses radiant light and energy to move rapidly across the battlefield. Her story adds another Radiant perspective to the growing roster of Protocol Agents.

VETO 
 - Veto is a mysterious Agent connected to the Protocol and is known for his ability to disrupt and neutralize other Agents' abilities. His powers make him particularly dangerous against Radiants and ability-focused opponents. His background is one of the newer parts of VALORANT's continuing lore.
