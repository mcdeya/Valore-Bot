1. BEFORE FIRST LIGHT
Radianite was not necessarily a completely new substance when First Light occurred. Evidence in the VALORANT lore indicates that Radianite and related phenomena existed in earlier periods of Earth's history.
During the Edo Period of Japan, from 1603 to 1876 CE, Radianite was known and used in some form. Yoru's samurai ancestor is confirmed to have lived during this period.
Ancient artifacts connected to Radianite have also been discovered. These discoveries indicate that Radianite-related events occurred long before the modern VALORANT era.

2. OMEN'S SPLIT
Before becoming the agent known as Omen, Omen worked as a hitman connected to the Scions of Hourglass.
He was sent on missions involving members of Kingdom Corporation. During an attempt to assassinate Viper, a traumatic incident involving Radianite occurred.
The incident resulted in Omen losing his previous physical form and becoming the mysterious, shadow-like figure known as Omen.
Omen's transformation is connected to the early history of the VALORANT Protocol.

3. FIRST LIGHT — 0 FL
First Light was a major global event that dramatically changed the world.
The event caused widespread disruption and was associated with a large-scale Radianite event. Electrical systems and power infrastructure around the world were affected.
After First Light, Radianite became extremely important to modern society.
First Light was followed by major developments in science, technology, energy production, and society.
Another major consequence was the appearance of people with extraordinary abilities. These people became known as Radiants.
The exact cause of First Light remains unknown.
Some evidence connects First Light with experiments involving Radianite, but the precise cause has not been confirmed.
First Light may also be connected to the existence of the different Earths in the VALORANT universe, although the exact relationship remains uncertain.

4. THE RISE OF RADIANITE
After First Light, Radianite became one of the most important resources in the world.
Radianite could be used for energy production, advanced technology, weapons, construction, and scientific research.
Kingdom Corporation became extremely powerful because of its ability to use and control Radianite.
Radianite technology contributed to rapid technological development throughout the world.
Radianite was also connected to the emergence of Radiants and their abilities.

5. THE RISE OF KINGDOM
Kingdom Corporation became one of the most powerful organizations on Alpha Earth.
After the emergence of Radianite, Kingdom expanded its influence through research, energy production, technology, and other industries.
Kingdom became heavily associated with Radianite and developed many technologies using the substance.
The corporation's facilities and research projects later became important locations and elements in VALORANT's story.

6. FORMATION OF THE VALORANT PROTOCOL
The VALORANT Protocol was formed in response to threats and consequences associated with First Light.
Brimstone and Viper were the founding members of the Protocol.
The organization recruited Agents with different backgrounds, abilities, and skills.
Some Agents are Radiants who possess supernatural abilities, while others rely on technology, weapons, training, or Radianite-powered equipment.
The Protocol's responsibilities changed over time.
Initially, the organization dealt with threats connected to the consequences of First Light.
Later, much of its attention became focused on conflicts involving Omega Earth and the VALORANT Legion.

7. THE EVERETT-LINDE PROJECT
The Everett-Linde facility was a point of cooperation between Kingdom organizations from Alpha Earth and Omega Earth.
Personnel from both worlds worked together at the facility.
Scientists Rúben Pontes from Omega Earth and Oran McEneff from Alpha Earth worked together on important research.
The facility later became connected to events involving sabotage and conflicts between the two Earths.

8. 10 aFL — THE RISE OF VENICE
Around ten years after First Light, Omega Earth carried out a major attack against Alpha Earth.
The event was shown in the DUELISTS cinematic.
Phoenix was involved in a mission in Venice and encountered Omega Jett.
Omega Jett was carrying a prototype Spike.
Phoenix attempted to stop Omega Jett, but she escaped and planted the Spike.
The Spike detonated and caused a massive Radianite-related disruption.
Part of Venice was lifted into the air, creating the floating area that later became associated with the Ascent map.
This event became known as the Rise of Venice or the Venice Incident.
The event increased public concern about Radianite and its potential dangers.
Kingdom received significant criticism because of the incident and its involvement with Radianite.

9. DUALITY — AFTER THE VENICE INCIDENT
The DUALITY cinematic takes place shortly after the Venice Incident.
The consequences of the Venice disaster received major public attention.
Kingdom attempted to defend its position regarding the safety of Radianite.
Public opinion toward Radianite and Radiants became more skeptical.
People began questioning whether Radianite and Radiant abilities represented a threat to humanity.
During this period, the VALORANT Protocol fought another attack from Omega Earth.
The attack occurred in Bind and involved Omega Phoenix.

10. 11 aFL — THE PROTOCOL'S SECRETS
Approximately one year after the Rise of Venice, information about the events began to spread publicly.
Images of Phoenix and Jett from the Venice Incident resurfaced on the internet.
Questions were also raised about the Everett-Linde facility.
Breach became concerned about maintaining the secrecy of the VALORANT Protocol.
The continued exposure of information increased pressure on the Protocol to protect its identity and operations.

11. FADE'S BLACKMAIL
Fade discovered confidential information about the VALORANT Protocol.
She believed that the Protocol was responsible for the loss of someone important to her.
Fade sent an email to Brimstone threatening to reveal the Protocol's confidential information.
Her blackmail campaign exposed some of the organization's hidden secrets and created difficulties for the Protocol.

12. THE PORTAL TO OMEGA EARTH
Killjoy and Neon worked on developing a functioning teleportal capable of reaching Omega Earth.
After continued work, the portal became operational.
Fade was sent through the portal on a reconnaissance mission.
Her mission was successful, and she was able to investigate the destination and return safely.
The discovery of another nearby portal terminal led the Protocol to prepare for a larger mission to Omega Earth.

13. SHATTERED — FIRST MISSION TO OMEGA EARTH

The VALORANT Protocol prepared its first major mission into Omega Earth.
Killjoy, Neon, and Reyna were selected for the mission.
Their objective was to gather information about Omega Earth's city and investigate Radianite storage locations.
The mission was intended to be a stealth operation.
However, the team was detected by ATLAS forces.
Omega Viper later joined the confrontation.
The team's entry portal was destroyed, forcing Neon and Reyna to defend the group while Killjoy completed her investigation.
The team eventually escaped into the city.
During the mission, the Protocol discovered that Omega Earth's version of the organization was not secretive.
Instead, the Omega organization, known as the VALORANT Legion, was publicly recognized and celebrated as a group of heroes.
The mission introduced Alpha Earth's VALORANT Protocol to the larger reality of Omega Earth.

14. THE VALORANT LEGION
The VALORANT Legion is the Omega Earth counterpart to Alpha Earth's VALORANT Protocol.
Unlike the secretive Alpha Protocol, the Legion is publicly known on Omega Earth.
The Legion is treated as a heroic organization by much of Omega Earth's population.
The conflict between the VALORANT Protocol and the VALORANT Legion is an important part of the continuing story.

15. LEGION'S NEW POWER SOURCE
Cypher investigated Omega Earth's activities and discovered that the VALORANT Legion had obtained a new source of power.
Further investigation by Cypher and Fade identified Varun Batra, also known as Harbor, as the keeper of the power source.
The Alpha Earth version of Varun Batra was working with REALM.
REALM is a multinational organization involved in recovering artifacts and returning them to their rightful locations.
The VALORANT Protocol became interested in Varun Batra as a possible recruit.

16. PROJECT LANDFALL
Project Landfall became another important part of the continuing conflict involving Alpha Earth, Omega Earth, Radianite, and the VALORANT Protocol.
The project is connected to the ongoing activities of Kingdom and the larger conflict between the two Earths.

17. GEKKO JOINS THE PROTOCOL
Gekko became a member of the VALORANT Protocol.
His recruitment expanded the Protocol's roster and introduced another Agent with unique abilities and a distinct background.

18. DEADLOCK JOINS THE PROTOCOL
Deadlock later joined the VALORANT Protocol.
Her recruitment expanded the organization's capabilities and introduced another Agent into the continuing conflict.

19. SUNSET
Sunset became another significant location in the VALORANT universe.
The location is connected to the continuing story of the VALORANT Protocol and the wider conflict involving Radianite and the two Earths.

20. THE RADIANT WAR — 200+ aFL
The Radiant War occurs in an alternate future at least 200 years after First Light.
In this future, humanity went to war against Radiants.
KAY/O was created during this conflict.
His purpose was to suppress and kill enemy Radiants.
The war resulted in enormous destruction around the world.
KAY/O eventually traveled back through time.
His goal was believed to be preventing the Radiant War from occurring.
KAY/O's time travel connects the distant future to the main timeline of VALORANT.

21. IMPORTANT TIMELINE CONCEPTS

FIRST LIGHT:
A major global event that changed the world and became the reference point for the VALORANT timeline.

FL:
A dating abbreviation associated with First Light.

aFL:
Means "after First Light."

ALPHA EARTH:
The Earth associated with the main VALORANT Protocol.

OMEGA EARTH:
A parallel Earth with its own version of the VALORANT organization.

VALORANT PROTOCOL:
The covert organization operating on Alpha Earth.

VALORANT LEGION:
The Omega Earth counterpart to the VALORANT Protocol.

RADIANITE:
A powerful substance associated with many technological, scientific, and supernatural developments.

RADIANTS:
Humans who possess extraordinary abilities.

THE SPIKE:
A Radianite-related device involved in conflicts between Alpha Earth and Omega Earth.
